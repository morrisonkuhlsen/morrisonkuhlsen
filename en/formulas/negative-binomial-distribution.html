Step by step

    What each term means

    Probability (P(X = k))
    The chance that success number r happens exactly on trial k, with k = r, r + 1, …
    Positions of the Other Successes (C(k − 1, r − 1))
    Trial k must be a success; the other r − 1 can be in any of the k − 1 earlier trials.
    Successes (pʳ)
    The chance of r successes, each with probability p, in independent trials.
    Failures ((1 − p)ᵏ⁻ʳ)
    The chance of the k − r failures along the way.
