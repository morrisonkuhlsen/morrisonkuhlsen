Example: a poll heard 600 young people and 400 older people. Approval was 62% among the young and 40% among the older, but the young are only 35% of the population. What is the estimated approval? Leave the respondents blank for an ordinary weighted mean.

Step by step

    What each term means

    Weighted Mean (x̄_w)
    A mean in which some values count more than others. With equal weights, it becomes the simple mean.
    Sum of the Weighted Values
    Each value multiplied by its weight, added up. Values with a larger weight contribute more.
    Sum of the Weights
    Dividing by the total weight brings the result back to the scale of the values. That is why weights can be counts, percentages or fractions: only their proportions matter.
