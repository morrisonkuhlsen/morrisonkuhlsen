    What each term means

    Smoothed Value (Sₜ)
    The estimate of the series' level at period t, and the forecast for t + 1.
    Smoothing Constant (α)
    Between 0 and 1: how much weight to give the new observation. A high α reacts fast and copies the noise; a low α is stable and slow to notice changes.
    Current Observation (xₜ)
    The value that has just arrived.
    Weight of the Past (1 − α)
    The weight the previous smoothed value keeps. Since it already blends the earlier ones, each old observation loses weight in a geometric progression.
    Previous Smoothed Value (Sₜ₋₁)
    The summary of the whole series up to the previous period. It is the method's memory: a single calculation per period, without storing the whole series.
