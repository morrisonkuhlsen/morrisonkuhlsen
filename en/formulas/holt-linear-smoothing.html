    What each term means

    Forecast (x̂ₙ₊ₕ)
    The value forecast h periods after the last one observed.
    Level (Lₙ)
    Where the series is now, without the noise. It is updated by blending the new value (weight α) with the previous forecast, level plus trend (weight 1 − α).
    Horizon (h)
    How many periods ahead. The forecast moves h steps along the trend.
    Trend (Tₙ)
    How much the series rises or falls per period. It is updated by blending the level's latest change (weight β) with the previous trend (weight 1 − β).
