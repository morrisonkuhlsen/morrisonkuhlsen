Step by step

    What each term means

    Confidence Interval
    The range of plausible values for the statistic in the population, obtained with no standard-error formula and no normality assumption.
    Lower Percentile
    The value below which α/2 of the resampled statistics θ̂* lie. At 95% confidence, the 2.5th percentile.
    Upper Percentile
    The value below which 1 − α/2 of the resampled statistics lie. At 95%, the 97.5th percentile. Between the two percentiles lie 95% of the resamples.
