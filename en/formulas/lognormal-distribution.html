Example: the time, in minutes, that visitors spend on a website has a normal logarithm, with μ = 1.5 and σ = 0.8. What is the chance that a visit lasts up to 10 minutes? Change the values to work out your own case.

Step by step

    What each term means

    Density (f(x))
    The height of the curve at x > 0. The curve starts at zero, rises fast and falls slowly, with a long right tail.
    One in the Numerator
    Together with the denominator, it makes the total area under the curve equal 1.
    Normalization (xσ√(2π))
    The σ√(2π) is the normal's; the extra x comes from the change of scale from ln x to x, which stretches small values and squeezes large ones.
    Bell on a Log Scale
    The normal's bell, but in ln x: μ and σ are the mean and standard deviation of the logarithm, and e^μ is the median of x.
