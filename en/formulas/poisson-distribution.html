    What each term means

    Probability of k Occurrences
    The probability that the event happens exactly 'k' times in an interval.
    Mean Rate (λ) to the Power k
    Lambda (λ) is the mean rate at which the event occurs. This term scales the rate to the specific number of occurrences 'k'.
    Exponential of the Negative Mean Rate
    Euler's number (e) raised to -λ. This term is the probability of no occurrences, the base of the calculation.
    k Factorial
    The factorial of the number of occurrences 'k'. It normalizes the distribution, accounting for the different ways the 'k' occurrences can be arranged.
