    What each term means

    Conditional Probability
    The probability of A happening given that B has already happened. It shows how the occurrence of B affects the probability of A.
    Probability of the Intersection
    The probability that both A and B happen. It is the overlap between events A and B.
    Probability of B
    The probability of event B. It normalizes the formula, keeping the conditional probability between 0 and 1.
