Step by step

    What each term means

    Sample Size (n)
    How many observations are needed to estimate the mean with the requested margin of error and confidence. The result is always rounded up.
    Squared Critical Value (z²)
    It comes from the confidence level: 1.645 for 90%, 1.96 for 95% and 2.576 for 99%. More confidence calls for more observations.
    Variance (σ²)
    How much the data vary. It usually comes from a pilot study or earlier research; the more scattered the data, the larger the sample.
    Squared Margin of Error (E²)
    The largest accepted gap between the sample mean and the true one, in the units of the data. Because it is squared, halving it quadruples the sample.
