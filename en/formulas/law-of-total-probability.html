Step by step

    What each term means

    Total Probability of B
    The overall probability of event B, taking into account every possible scenario or partition of the sample space.
    Summation
    Adds over all the partitions A₁, A₂, ..., Aₙ, which together form a complete and mutually exclusive partition of the sample space.
    Conditional Probability P(B|Aᵢ)
    The probability of B given that Aᵢ happened. It is the probability of B within each specific scenario Aᵢ.
    Probability of Aᵢ
    The probability of each partition Aᵢ. The events Aᵢ must be mutually exclusive and exhaustive (their union covers the whole sample space).
