    What each term means

    Arrangement (A(n, k))
    How many ordered sequences of k different items can be formed from n items: podiums, passwords without repeats, queues.
    Factorial of n (n!)
    Every possible order of the n items: n · (n − 1) · … · 1. When k = n, the arrangement is the permutation, n!.
    Factorial of What Is Left ((n − k)!)
    Cancels the factors of the items left out. What remains is n · (n − 1) · … · (n − k + 1): n options for the first position, one fewer for each next one.
