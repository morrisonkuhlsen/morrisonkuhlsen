Step by step

    What each term means

    Slope (m)
    How much y changes for each unit of x. Positive, the line rises; negative, it falls; zero, it is horizontal. With it and a point comes the intercept b = y₁ − m x₁, and the line is y = mx + b.
    How Much y Rises (y₂ − y₁)
    The vertical change between the two points.
    How Far x Moves (x₂ − x₁)
    The horizontal change. If it is zero, the line is vertical and the slope doesn't exist.
