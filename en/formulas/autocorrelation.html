Step by step

    What each term means

    Autocorrelation at Lag k (rₖ)
    The correlation of the series with itself k periods later, from −1 to 1. Positive at k = 1 means inertia; a spike at k = 4 or 12, seasonality.
    Lagged Products
    Multiplies each period's deviation by the deviation k periods ahead. If high values tend to be followed, k periods later, by high values, the sum comes out positive.
    Total Variation
    The sum of the squared deviations of the whole series. It is the same denominator for every lag, which keeps rₖ between −1 and 1.

    When to use it, assumptions and alternatives: the Ljung–Box test in the statistical tests catalog.
