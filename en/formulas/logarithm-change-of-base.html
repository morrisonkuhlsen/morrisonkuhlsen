Step by step

    What each term means

    Logarithm in Base b
    The exponent that takes b to x: if b to the power L gives x, then log_b x = L. It answers how many times to multiply b by itself.
    Natural Log of x (ln x)
    The logarithm in base e ≈ 2.718, the one in calculators and programming languages. Any base works here, as long as it is the same above and below.
    Natural Log of the Base (ln b)
    The scale factor: dividing by ln b converts from base e to base b. Logarithms in different bases are proportional.
