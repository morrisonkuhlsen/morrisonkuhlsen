Example: expand (a + b)⁵ with a = 0.7 and b = 0.3. Since a + b = 1, each term is a binomial probability, and they add up to 1. Change the values to work out your own case.

Step by step

    What each term means

    The Power ((a + b)ⁿ)
    The product of n brackets (a + b). Multiplying everything out, each term picks a or b from each bracket.
    Sum from k = 0 to n
    One term for each number k of b's picked: n + 1 terms in all.
    Binomial Coefficient (C(n, k))
    How many ways to pick the k brackets that give b. These are the numbers in row n of Pascal's triangle.
    Power of a (aⁿ⁻ᵏ)
    The remaining n − k brackets give a.
    Power of b (bᵏ)
    The k brackets picked give b. The exponents of a and b always add up to n.
