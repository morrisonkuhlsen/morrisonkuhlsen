Step by step

    What each term means

    Entropy (H)
    The average uncertainty about the outcome, in bits. Zero when the outcome is certain; highest, log₂ k, when the k outcomes are equally likely.
    Sum with the Sign Flipped (−Σ)
    A sum over all outcomes. The minus sign is there because the log of a probability is negative, and the entropy should come out positive.
    Probability (pᵢ)
    The weight of each outcome: the average is weighted by the chance of each one happening.
    Surprise (log₂ pᵢ)
    −log₂ p is an outcome's surprise: 1 bit for a chance of 1/2, 3 bits for 1/8. Rare outcomes surprise more.
