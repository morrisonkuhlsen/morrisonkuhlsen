Step by step

    What each term means

    General Term (aₙ)
    The term at position n, computed directly, without listing the earlier ones.
    First Term (a₁)
    Where the sequence starts.
    First Difference (Δ₁)
    a₂ − a₁, the first jump. In an arithmetic sequence, the jumps are all equal and the formula would stop here.
    Second Difference (Δ₂) and Its Weight
    How much each jump grows over the previous one. Up to position n, it builds up (n − 1)(n − 2)/2 times — a triangular number.
