    What each term means

    Absolute Value Inequality
    |x − a| is the distance between x and a on the line, without sign. Asking for it to be at most r is asking for the x close to a.
    Lower Bound (a − r)
    The leftmost point still within distance r of the centre.
    Upper Bound (a + r)
    The rightmost point. With ≥ instead of ≤, the solution becomes the outside: x ≤ a − r or x ≥ a + r.
