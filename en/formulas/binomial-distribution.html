Step by step

    What each term means

    Probability of k Successes
    The probability of getting exactly 'k' successes in 'n' independent trials, each with probability 'p' of success.
    Binomial Coefficient
    The number of different ways to choose 'k' successes out of 'n' trials. Computed as C(n, k) = n! / (k! · (n-k)!).
    Probability of Success (p^k)
    The probability of success 'p' on each trial, raised to the power 'k'. It is the probability that k specific trials are all successes.
    Probability of Failure ((1-p)^(n-k))
    The probability of failure (1-p) raised to the power (n-k). It is the probability of (n-k) failures in the remaining trials.
