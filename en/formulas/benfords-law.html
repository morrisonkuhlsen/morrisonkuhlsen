    What each term means

    Chance of the First Digit (P(d))
    The probability that a number starts with digit d, from 1 to 9: about 30% for 1 and under 5% for 9.
    Base-10 Logarithm
    Measures where the number falls within its decade, on a logarithmic ruler. It is on that ruler that numbers spread over several orders of magnitude become uniform.
    From d to d + 1 ((1 + 1/d))
    The piece of the decade between d and d + 1, measured in logarithm: from 1 to 2 is log₁₀ 2 ≈ 30%; from 9 to 10, only log₁₀(10/9) ≈ 4.6%.
