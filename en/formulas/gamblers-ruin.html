Step by step

    What each term means

    Chance of Reaching the Target (Pᵢ)
    Starting with i, the probability of reaching N before reaching zero, winning or losing 1 per round.
    Numerator
    q/p is the ratio of losing to winning a round. With q/p > 1 the game is unfavourable, and (q/p)ⁱ grows fast with the capital.
    Denominator
    The same calculation for the target N. Since N > i, in an unfavourable game the denominator is far larger than the numerator, and the chance shrinks. With p = q, the formula becomes i/N.
