Example: 3 kg of cheese cost 45. How much do 5 kg cost? Switch to inverse proportion and read it as: 3 builders take 45 days; how many days would 5 take? Change the values to work out your own case.

Step by step

    What each term means

    Value Sought (y₂)
    The value of y that matches the new x₂.
    Known Value (y₁)
    The y of the known case.
    New x (x₂)
    The new quantity. In a direct proportion, y grows by the same ratio as x: x₂/x₁.
    Known x (x₁)
    The x of the known case. In an inverse proportion, the fraction flips, y₂ = y₁ · x₁/x₂, because the product x · y stays constant.
