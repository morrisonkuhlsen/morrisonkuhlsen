Example: in a study of kidney stones, treatment A cured 81 of 87 patients with small stones and 192 of 263 with large stones; treatment B, 234 of 270 and 55 of 80. A is better in both groups. And overall? Change the values to work out your own case.

Step by step

    What each term means

    Overall Rate
    The success rate pooling every group, for one treatment. It is the one that can tell a story opposite to the groups'.
    Sum over the Groups
    Adds each group's contribution, such as small stones and large stones.
    Group Weight (n_g/N)
    The share of that treatment's cases that fell into group g. When A and B are spread differently across the groups, the weights differ, and that is where the reversal comes from.
    Rate in the Group (p_g)
    The treatment's success rate within group g. Comparing rates within each group neutralizes the group's influence.
