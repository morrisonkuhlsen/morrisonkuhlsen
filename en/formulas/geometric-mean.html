    What each term means

    Geometric Mean (G)
    The value that, multiplied by itself n times, gives the same product as the data. It is the right average for factors that multiply, such as growth rates and indices.
    Root Index (n)
    The number of values. The n-th root undoes the product of n factors, just as dividing by n undoes the sum in the arithmetic mean.
    Product (Πxᵢ)
    The product of all the values: x₁ · x₂ · … · xₙ. For many values, it is safer to add up the logarithms, which gives the same result without the calculation blowing up.
