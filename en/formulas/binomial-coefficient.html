Step by step

    What each term means

    Binomial Coefficient
    The number of ways to choose 'k' elements from a set of 'n' elements, regardless of order.
    Total Items (n!)
    The factorial of the total number of items available in the set we are choosing from.
    Items Chosen (k!)
    The factorial of the number of items we want to choose from the full set of 'n'.
    Items Not Chosen ((n-k)!)
    The factorial of the number of items left in the set after choosing 'k' of them.
