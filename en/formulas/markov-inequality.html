Step by step

    What each term means

    Chance of High Values (P(X ≥ a))
    The probability that the variable reaches a or goes beyond it. Markov gives a ceiling for it, not the exact value.
    Mean (E[X])
    The only information the inequality needs, besides the variable never being negative.
    Threshold (a)
    The larger the threshold, the lower the ceiling. With a equal to 5 times the mean, at most 20% of the values get there.
