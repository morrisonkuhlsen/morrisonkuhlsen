    What each term means

    Sum of the First n Terms (Sₙ)
    The total of a + ar + ar² + … + arⁿ⁻¹: n terms, each one the previous multiplied by the ratio r.
    First Term (a)
    The term that opens the sequence. All the others are it multiplied by powers of r.
    Numerator (1 − rⁿ)
    It comes from a trick: multiplying the sum by r, almost every term repeats, and the difference Sₙ − rSₙ leaves only a − arⁿ. When |r| < 1, rⁿ goes to zero and the numerator becomes 1.
    Denominator (1 − r)
    It completes the trick: Sₙ(1 − r) = a(1 − rⁿ). With r = 1 the formula doesn't hold, and the sum is simply n · a. With |r| < 1, the infinite sum is a / (1 − r).
