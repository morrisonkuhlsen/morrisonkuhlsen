    What each term means

    Density (f(x))
    Constant between a and b and zero outside: no value in the interval is likelier than another.
    One in the Numerator
    The total area under the density must be 1, the certainty that the value lands somewhere.
    Width of the Interval (b − a)
    The wider the interval, the lower the density, so the area stays 1. The chance of a stretch is its width divided by b − a.
