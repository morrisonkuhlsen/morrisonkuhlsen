Step by step

    What each term means

    Density (f(x))
    The height of the curve at x, for x ≥ 0. It is not a probability: probabilities are areas under the curve, such as P(X ≤ x) = 1 − e^(−λx).
    Rate (λ)
    How many events happen, on average, per unit of time or distance. The mean wait between them is 1/λ: with 3 customers an hour, 20 minutes.
    Exponential Decay (e^(−λx))
    The chance of getting past x with no event, P(X > x). It drops by the same fraction for each extra unit of waiting — hence the distribution's lack of memory.
