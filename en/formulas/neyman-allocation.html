Step by step

    What each term means

    Sample of Stratum h (nₕ)
    The sample size to draw from a specific stratum 'h'. It is the value the formula computes.
    Total Sample Size (n)
    The total number of individuals in the final sample, adding up the samples of all strata.
    Population of Stratum h (Nₕ)
    The total number of individuals in stratum 'h' of the population.
    Standard Deviation of Stratum h (σₕ)
    The standard deviation of the variable of interest within stratum 'h'. Strata with more variability get a larger sample.
    Weighted Sum (Σ Nₕσₕ)
    The sum of each stratum's population size (Nₕ) times its standard deviation (σₕ), over all strata. It works as a normalizing factor.
