Step by step

    What each term means

    Positive Predictive Value (PPV)
    The chance of being ill given a positive test. It is what matters to whoever gets the result, and it depends heavily on how common the disease is.
    True Positives (S · p)
    The share of the population that is ill and tests positive: the sensitivity S (how many ill people the test catches) times the prevalence p.
    All Positives
    The true positives plus the false ones: healthy people, 1 − p, who test positive by mistake, 1 − E, where E is the specificity. When the disease is rare, the false ones can be the majority.
