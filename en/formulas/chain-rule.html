    What each term means

    Derivative of the Composite
    The rate of change of f(g(x)): one function applied to the result of another, such as sin(x²) or e^(3x + 1).
    Outer Derivative (f′(g(x)))
    Differentiate the outer function as if the inner one were a single variable, then put g(x) back in its place.
    Inner Derivative (g′(x))
    The correction for the inner function's speed: if g changes fast, the composite changes proportionally faster.
