Example: two radiologists rated the same 100 scans as normal or abnormal. They agreed on 40 normal and 45 abnormal; on 15, they disagreed. How much do they agree beyond what chance would explain? Change the values to work out your own case.

Step by step

    What each term means

    Kappa (κ)
    Agreement beyond chance, from −1 to 1: 1 is perfect agreement, 0 is what two raters guessing with the same frequencies would get, and negative values are worse than chance.
    Observed Agreement (pₒ)
    The share of cases where both gave the same rating: the sum of the diagonal divided by the total.
    Chance Agreement (pₑ)
    The agreement that would come from each rater's frequencies alone, if they rated independently. If both call almost everything normal, they agree a lot even without any criterion.
    Room Beyond Chance (1 − pₑ)
    How much agreement there was left to win beyond chance. Kappa is the share of it that was achieved.
