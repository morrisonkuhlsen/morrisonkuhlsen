Example: two classes took the same exam. Class A, with 20 students, had a mean of 72 and a standard deviation of 10; class B, with 22 students, a mean of 65 and a standard deviation of 12. Is the difference real, or could it be chance? Change the values to work out your own case.

Step by step

    What each term means

    t Statistic
    How many standard errors separate the two means. Compared with the t distribution, it gives the p-value: the chance of a difference like this by chance alone, if the true means were equal.
    Difference of the Means (x̄₁ − x̄₂)
    What was observed: how far one group's mean landed above the other's, in the units of the data.
    Standard Error of the Difference
    How much the difference of the means varies from sample to sample. It adds the variances of the two means without assuming the groups have the same variance — which is what defines Welch's test.
