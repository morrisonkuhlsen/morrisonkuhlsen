Step by step

    What each term means

    Sample Mean (X̄ₙ)
    The mean of a sample of size 'n' drawn from the population. The theorem describes the distribution of these means.
    Is Distributed As (~)
    Means that, as the sample size 'n' grows, the distribution of sample means approaches a normal distribution.
    Normal Distribution (N)
    A continuous, bell-shaped probability distribution defined by its mean and variance.
    Population Mean (μ)
    The mean of the whole population. The distribution of sample means is centered on this value.
    Population Variance (σ²)
    A measure of how spread out the data are across the whole population.
    Sample Size (n)
    The number of observations in each sample. The variance of the distribution of sample means shrinks as 'n' grows.
