Step by step

    What each term means

    Probability of k
    The probability of getting exactly 'k' in a single Bernoulli trial, where k can be 0 (failure) or 1 (success).
    Probability of Success (p^k)
    The probability of success 'p' raised to the power 'k'. When k = 1 it gives p (success); when k = 0 it gives 1.
    Probability of Failure ((1-p)^(1-k))
    The probability of failure (1-p) raised to the power (1-k). When k = 0 it gives (1-p) (failure); when k = 1 it gives 1.
