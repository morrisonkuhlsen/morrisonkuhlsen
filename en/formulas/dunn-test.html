Step by step

    What each term means

    Pair z Statistic
    How many standard errors separate two groups' mean ranks. Compared with the normal, it gives the pair's p-value, which is then adjusted for the number of comparisons.
    Difference in Mean Ranks
    The ranks are the same as in Kruskal-Wallis: all values sorted together.
    Standard Error
    It depends on the total N and the size of the two groups. With ties, a correction term is subtracted.
