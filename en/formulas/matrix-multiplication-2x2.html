Example: A stretches the x axis to double, and B rotates the plane 90° anticlockwise. What does AB do — rotate and then stretch? And BA? Change the matrices to work out your own case.

Step by step

    What each term means

    Entry of the Product ((AB)ᵢⱼ)
    The entry in row i and column j of AB. There are four, one for each pairing of a row of A with a column of B.
    First Pair
    The first entry of row i of A times the first of column j of B.
    Second Pair
    The second times the second. Adding the two pairs is the dot product of the row and the column.
