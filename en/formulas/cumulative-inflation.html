Step by step

    What each term means

    Cumulative Inflation (I)
    How much prices rose over the whole period, relative to the start.
    Product of the Factors
    Each rate becomes a factor: 0.5% becomes 1.005. The factors are multiplied, because each rise applies to prices that have already risen — it is interest on interest.
    Minus 1
    The product says by how much prices were multiplied; subtracting 1 leaves the rise. A factor of 1.045 is 4.5% inflation.
