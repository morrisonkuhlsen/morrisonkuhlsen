    What each term means

    Logit (Log of the Odds)
    The odds p/(1 − p) compare the probability of y = 1 with that of y = 0; the logarithm stretches that from 0–infinity to the whole line, where a linear model fits.
    Intercept (β₀)
    The logit when x = 0. It sets where the S curve sits.
    Coefficient (β₁ x)
    How much the logit changes per unit of x. e to the β₁ is the odds ratio: the factor that multiplies the odds with each unit.
