Step by step

    What each term means

    Density Function (f(x))
    The relative likelihood that a continuous random variable takes a specific value 'x'.
    Normalizing Coefficient
    This term makes the total area under the curve equal to 1. It is built from the standard deviation (σ) and 2π.
    Exponential Function
    The heart of the distribution, where 'e' is the base of the natural logarithm. The exponent depends on how far 'x' is from the mean (μ), measured in standard deviations (σ).
