Step by step

    What each term means

    Coefficient of Determination (R²)
    The share of the variation in y that the line explains, from 0 to 1. R² = 0.9 means 90% of the variation goes away once x is taken into account.
    One Minus the Residual Share
    Everything except what the line doesn't explain. Since the total variation splits exactly into an explained part and a residual part, subtracting the residual share from 1 gives the explained share.
    Residual Sum of Squares
    The variation left after the line: the distance from each y to its predicted value ŷ, squared. It is what the least-squares line makes as small as possible.
    Total Sum of Squares
    The variation of y around its own mean, without using x. It is the yardstick: how much there would be to explain.
