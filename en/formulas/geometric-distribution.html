Step by step

    What each term means

    Probability of the First Success (P(X = k))
    The chance that the first success happens exactly on trial k, with k = 1, 2, 3, …
    Failures Before the Success ((1 − p)ᵏ⁻¹)
    The chance that the first k − 1 trials all fail. Since the trials are independent, the failure chances multiply.
    Probability of Success (p)
    The chance of success on each trial, the same on all of them. It closes the sequence: the success, at last, on trial k.
