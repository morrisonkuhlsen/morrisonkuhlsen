Formulas

25 statistics and probability formulas explained term by term. Open one and tap each piece to see what it means.

Probability

  • Bayes' Theorem Updates the probability of a hypothesis after seeing evidence.
  • Conditional Probability The chance of A when you already know B happened.
  • Law of Total Probability Adds up the probability of B across every scenario of a partition.
  • Binomial Coefficient How many ways there are to choose k items out of n, ignoring order.
  • Chebyshev's Theorem A bound on the share of data far from the mean, for any distribution.

Distributions

  • Bernoulli Distribution A single trial with two outcomes: success or failure.
  • Binomial Distribution The number of successes in n independent trials.
  • Poisson Distribution The number of events in an interval, given a mean rate.
  • Normal (Gaussian) Distribution The bell curve defined by its mean and standard deviation.
  • Central Limit Theorem Why the sample mean tends to a normal distribution as n grows.

Inference

  • Z-Score How many standard deviations an observation is from the mean.
  • Student's t-Test Compares a sample mean with a hypothesized value when σ is unknown.
  • Confidence Interval A range of plausible values for the population mean.
  • Statistical Power How many observations a t-test needs to detect an effect.
  • Chi-Square Test (χ²) Measures the gap between observed and expected frequencies.

Sampling

  • Cochran's Formula Sample size to estimate a proportion with a given margin of error.
  • Margin of Error The "plus or minus" of a poll, from the sample size.
  • Finite Population Correction Shrinks the sample size when the population is small.
  • Neyman Allocation Splits the sample across strata by size and variability.

Association and means

  • Covariance Whether two variables move together, and in which direction.
  • Pearson Correlation Coefficient (r) Strength and direction of a linear relationship, from −1 to 1.
  • Simple Linear Regression The line that predicts y from x.
  • Harmonic Mean The right average for rates and ratios.

Algebra and calculus

  • Quadratic Formula The roots of ax² + bx + c = 0.
  • L'Hôpital's Rule Resolves indeterminate limits of the form 0/0 or ∞/∞.
