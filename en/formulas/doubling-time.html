Step by step

    What each term means

    Doubling Time (T)
    How many periods until the value doubles. It doesn't depend on the starting value: from 100 to 200 takes as long as from 1,000 to 2,000.
    Log of 2 (ln 2 ≈ 0.693)
    The target: doubling. To triple, it would be ln 3; to halve, ln ½, and T becomes the half-life.
    Log of the Factor (ln(1 + r))
    How much the value grows per period, on the log scale. For small rates, ln(1 + r) ≈ r, which gives the rule of 70: T ≈ 70 / r%.
