Step by step

    What each term means

    Interval for the Variance
    The range of plausible values for the population variance σ². Taking the square root of the bounds gives the interval for the standard deviation σ.
    Sum of Squares ((n − 1)s²)
    The sample's total variation. With normal data, (n − 1)s²/σ² follows a chi-square with n − 1 degrees of freedom — that is where the interval comes from.
    Upper Critical Value
    The chi-square value that leaves α/2 above it. Being in the denominator, it gives the lower bound of the interval.
    Lower Critical Value
    The one that leaves α/2 below it, and gives the upper bound. Since the chi-square is skewed, the interval isn't centred on s².
