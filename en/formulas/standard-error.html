    What each term means

    Standard Error (SE)
    The standard deviation of the sample mean: how much the mean of n values varies from one sample to the next. It is the piece that goes into confidence intervals and tests for the mean.
    Standard Deviation (σ)
    How much each individual value varies around the population mean. When σ is unknown, the sample standard deviation s is used.
    Square Root of the Sample Size (√n)
    Means of more values vary less, because deviations up and down offset each other. But the gain goes by the root: halving the error takes four times as much data.
