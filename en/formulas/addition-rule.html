Example: in a class, 60% of the students like maths, 45% like physics and 30% like both. Picking a student at random, what is the chance they like at least one? Change the values to work out your own case.

Step by step

    What each term means

    Union (P(A ∪ B))
    The chance that A happens, or B, or both: at least one of them.
    Probability of A
    Includes the cases where only A happens and those where A and B happen together.
    Probability of B
    Also includes the intersection. Adding P(A) and P(B) counts the cases of both twice.
    Intersection (P(A ∩ B))
    The chance of both happening together. Subtracting it once undoes the double count. If A and B can't happen together, it is zero and the union is a plain sum.
