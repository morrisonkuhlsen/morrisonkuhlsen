Which statistical test should I use?

Start with the first question and follow the arrows until you reach a test. The test at the end leads to its page, with the null hypothesis, assumptions, formula and R and Python code. All tests are in the statistical tests catalog.

Before following the tree

  • Normal or not? With large samples, tests of means tolerate non-normality well. When in doubt, look at the histogram and the Q-Q plot before relying on a normality test alone.
  • Independent or paired? If each value in one group has a natural partner in the other, such as the same patient before and after, the samples are paired.
  • The tree is a starting point. It covers the usual cases; the study design and the research question may call for another test.
