Explore the table Which test should I use?

How to find a test. Each row is a family and each column a study design; the block on the right gathers the tests that do not depend on how many groups there are. Tap a test to see its sheet.

How to read it

  • Number and symbol follow the reading order of the table; the symbol recalls the test statistic.
  • Under the name is the reference distribution used for the p-value.
  • Means × ranks: in the same column, the ranks row has the alternative that does not assume normality.
  • Gaps are crossings without a test in common use.

To do the math, see the interactive formulas and the Z, t, F and χ² tables.
