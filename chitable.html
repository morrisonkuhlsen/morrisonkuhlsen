    Critical χ² value

    χ² = F⁻¹(1 − α)

    α must be between 0 and 1, and ν at least 1.

    18.3070 critical χ²

    Confidence interval for a variance

    [(n − 1)s² / χ²(α/2), (n − 1)s² / χ²(1 − α/2)]

    Need s > 0, n ≥ 2 and a confidence level between 0.5 and 1.
